Why bother? Moments explain why a small force at a long lever moves a huge load. Welcome to rigid bodies.

1. New chapter, new idea

Particles only need ∑F = 0. Rigid bodies can rotate, so we also track moments. A moment measures a force’s tendency to rotate a body about a point or axis.

2. Moment of a force about a point

In 2D, M = F × (perpendicular lever arm), with a consistent sign convention (CCW positive is common in class). Vector form: M = r × F, where r runs from the moment center to any point on the force’s line of action.

4. Couples

A couple is two equal, opposite, non-collinear forces. Its moment is the same about every point—pure torque with zero net force. That is why we can slide couples around an FBD carefully when replacing force systems.
