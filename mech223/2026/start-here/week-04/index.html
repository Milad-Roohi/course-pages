Why bother? The third dimension is not scarier—it is one more component. Unit vectors keep you honest.

1. What changes in 3D

Same idea as 2D—forces are vectors—but now we use three components. Direction can be given by angles, by unit vectors along a line between two points, or by direction cosines.

2. Position and unit vectors

If a force of magnitude F acts from point A toward B, form rAB = rB − rA, then u = rAB / |rAB|, and F = F u. Sign errors almost always come from swapping start and end points.
